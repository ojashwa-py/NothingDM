Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

InstaAI Automation ("we", "our", or "us") provides a private Instagram comment-to-DM automation engine. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our platform and service.

1. Information We Process

When integrated with Instagram Graph API, our application processes:

  • Public comments posted on your authorized Instagram Reels and Posts.
  • Public Instagram usernames associated with processed comments.
  • Comment IDs and timestamp metadata required for event deduplication and logging.

2. How We Use Data

Data processed by InstaAI Automation is strictly used for:

  • Evaluating comment content against user-configured keyword or AI rules using Google Gemini API.
  • Automating private Direct Message (DM) responses and public comment replies via Meta Instagram Graph API.
  • Displaying live activity metrics on your private dashboard.

3. Data Storage & Security

All database logs and configuration settings are stored securely within Google Cloud Firestore. We do not sell, rent, or share any collected data with third parties.

4. Third-Party API Services

InstaAI Automation integrates with official Meta APIs (Instagram Graph API) and Google Gemini AI API. Data processed via these services is subject to Meta's Data Policy and Google's Generative AI Privacy Terms.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Activity logs are automatically retained for diagnostic and analytics purposes. You may clear your logs or revoke access to your Meta Instagram token at any time through the dashboard or your Meta Business integration settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via your official project administrator dashboard.
